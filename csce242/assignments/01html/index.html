The two best Sports drinks

Blue powerade is undeniably the best tasting sports drink on the market. It hydrates you very well. It contains electrolytes and has a sugar free option. It is one of the best selling powerades in their lineup.

Yellow Gatorade is a close second. It has been a staple of the gatorade company for decades. It contains electrolytes and is available in multiple sizes. It is the most refreshing drink in my opinion.

Benefits of Sports Drinks

  • They help replace electrolytes.
  • They help you rehydrate after working out
  • They are cheap and have a lot of flavors
